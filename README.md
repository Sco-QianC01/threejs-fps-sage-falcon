DUSTLINE is a round-based tactical FPS in three.js: VANGUARD attacks a container yard against ROOK, planting a device at site A or B or eliminating the defenders, with a buy phase, an economy, and bots that hold sites, rotate, and react to what they see and hear. First to 5 rounds takes the match.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Click to deploy, press B during the freeze to buy weapons, and hold E at a site to plant the device.
